   1. Monotone dose-response across independent round-number gates
      (59.4 -> 64.4 -> 71.8 -> 72.2 -> 73.6 -> 75.4) with break-even cleared

      70.0/73.3/77.4 — no third below 63%, no single-window artifact.
   3. NOT an extremity proxy (§5): within req/avg 1.04-1.20, cooling 81.5% vs
      60.0% (+21.5pp); within req/avg >= 1.20, cooling 70.3% vs heating-side
      48.8% (ROI -9.7%). The audit's "strong margin" bucket was carried by
      cooling games; margin alone is not the driver.

      CYBER 67.7->85.7, KBL 90.0 (N=6), NBA 100 (N=5).
   5. Counterfactual: dropped alerts are 47.7-54.8% accurate (coin flips);
      the gate discards variance, not edge. Net units RISE despite ~half the stakes.
