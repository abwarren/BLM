E. ROOT-CAUSE CONCLUSION — "Why are BLM alerts becoming more accurate?"

 1. CONFIRMED (DB-measured):
    * TIER SWITCH: the 50% checkpoint (55.34% CLEAN / 58.08% ALL — barely above the
      49.83% baseline) was removed on 2026-09-14; all production alerts now come from
      the 75% tier (66.94% ALL). Average served-alert accuracy rose ~10pp mechanically.
    * SELECTIVITY COLLAPSE: gates (live-market-only 09-12, ≤300s freshness, point-in-time
      reference, strict ×1.04 margin) cut the trigger rate from 24.5% of games to <1.2%.
      Fewer, more extreme alerts -> higher hit rate. Volume fell ~25x.
    * STALE-LINE PARADOX: alerts on frozen/stale lines hit 75.11% vs 59.38% CLEAN —
      the raw DB (checkpoint_market, all checkpoints: 7119W/2147L = 76.8%) therefore
      OVERSTATES what production serves today; part of the perceived improvement is
      stale rows (excluded live since 09-12) dominating historical counts.
    * THE GAMES DID NOT CHANGE: baselines flat across eras (52.7/50.0/51.2) and
      windows (47.2–54.0, no trend). Zero leakage found — the improvement is not
      look-ahead, settlement drift, or reference contamination.
 2. PLAUSIBLE (needs more N):
    * Momentum at fire time is the strongest in-data driver (cooling 73.6% vs
      heating 51.0%) — a real conditioning variable, not yet in the rule.
    * Late progress / <6min remaining hits 76.5% (small N).
    * Extreme-gap league tail (NBA +28 pts/min required gap, 7/7) is real but fragile.
    * Q4-discount dose-response (46% -> 80% up to +25%) suggests quarter-line capture
      would add a genuine signal — currently UNMEASURABLE (no quarter lines collected).
 3. NOT CONTRIBUTING:
    * League-pace regime drift (flat baselines).
    * Required/league margin magnitude beyond the 1.04 gate (non-monotonic hit rates).
    * Absolute line level, and |fair−line| size (mildly NEGATIVE — bigger deviations
      fare slightly worse).
